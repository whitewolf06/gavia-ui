source graph. CSS, images, font binaries/data and Python are outside Knip's

## Seven retained PT type-name findings

All seven declarations in `packages/ui-kit/src/pt-types.ts` are referenced by
section types in `WlPtMap`. Knip reports that their names are not imported from
another module; it does not report their definitions as unused within this file.
Their exported names are also present in the immutable
`tests/fixtures/compatibility-0.11.1/declarations/pt-types.d.ts`.

- `WlPtCheckboxContext`
- `WlPtCheckedContext`
- `WlPtOptionContext`
- `WlPtFocusedContext`
- `WlPtActiveContext`
- `WlPtSelectedContext`
- `WlPtDayContext`

They describe typed PT callback state, including checked/indeterminate,
focused/selected, active/disabled and calendar-day state. Removing these exports
would alter existing library declarations. This internal audit does not authorize
that compatibility change. All seven remain explicitly recorded rather than
being deleted, renamed or hidden in audit configuration.
